BACKGROUND & ETHOS

My engineering journey began with a clear curiosity for how code creates immediate human utility. Mobile operating systems represent the most personal interface between users and software — where milliseconds of latency or unexpected crashes directly affect user trust.

In commercial Android production, I stepped into live codebases serving high user volumes. Working on apps with hundreds of thousands to 5 Million+ downloads instilled a disciplined respect for memory leaks, lifecycle events, battery conservation, and Google Play compliance.
