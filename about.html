BACKGROUND & DRIVE

From Writing First Lines to Engineering Production Scale

My journey began with a curiosity for how software translates into meaningful human experiences. Starting from foundational programming principles, I quickly realized that mobile screens are where users interact most intimately with technology.

Transitioning into commercial Android engineering, I worked on applications deployed on Google Play that rapidly scaled to hundreds of thousands and then millions of downloads. This taught me that building for scale requires deep respect for memory leaks, battery consumption, network latency, and intuitive UX.

Alongside my engineering work, I run educational initiatives—teaching Computer Science and creating tutorials on CodeCraftX. Mentoring others has sharpened my ability to communicate intricate technical architectures with crystal-clear simplicity.
